How to play

Aliens

Start as a dretch, the smallest alien. Kill humans to earn evos, then evolve: basilisk, marauder, dragoon, up to the tyrant. Builders grow the base around the Overmind – without it, nothing new spawns.

Humans

Spawn with a rifle and a construction kit. Credits from kills buy shotguns, lasguns, flamers, armour and jetpacks at the armoury. The Reactor powers everything; turrets and medistations keep the base alive.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Tremulous is free and open source, and so is this edition. No ads in the game, no account.

Does it work on my phone?

It runs on recent phones and tablets with Chrome, Safari or Firefox; on-screen touch controls are in the works. The first start loads about 250 MB of game data, after that it comes from the browser cache.

Can I play with friends on the original game?

Browsers can't open the UDP connections the desktop game uses, so these servers are web-only. Everyone just opens this page.

Is there always a match?

Yes. When a server is short of people, AI players take the free seats so every round is a full one, and they step aside as more players join.

Who is behind this?

Tremulous was made by Dark Legion Development and kept alive by GrangerHub. Justin Luong and Auriga ported it to the web. WebNova360 runs these servers, the AI players and the fixes. Everything is on GitHub.
